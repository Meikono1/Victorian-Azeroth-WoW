## Version 3 – Quel'Thalas

The first big content update since the restart. Quel'Thalas, the Ghostlands and Zul'Aman now tell their own story, and the mod runs on Victoria 3 1.15.

### Silvermoon
- **Mana:** a new good the elves cannot live without. Ley Line Taps draw it from the land, and the journal entry "The Mana Hunger" decides each month whether your people thrive or wither. Until enough taps stand, you can disenchant heirlooms, ration Mana or drain the fauna of Eversong, each at a price.
- **The Dead Scar:** the path of the Scourge still blights the land and feeds Deathholme. Heal it region by region with costly purification rituals.
- **Journal entries:** drive out Deathholme, raze Zul'Aman, and open the road to Tranquillien stage by stage, or take it by force.
- **The Wretched:** fallen elves with a country of their own and a hunger that only grows.
  - Offer them a Mana Pact, then heal them over years or absorb them at once and live with the consequences.
  - Put bounties on their three leaders.
  - Beware their raids, their revenge and, when the threat peaks, their frenzy.
  - If your own people starve for Mana for too long, new Wretched may rise.
- **Characters:** Lor'themar Theron rules as Regent Lord, and Halduron Brightwing, Grand Magister Rommath and Lady Liadrin each strengthen your journal entries.

### Zul'Jarra and the Amani
- **Gather the Tribes:** bring the Shadowpine home with tribute and patience, and send envoys to the Mossflayer.
- **Malacrass and the Loa:** four rituals. Bind each animal god for great power or honor it for a lesser blessing, but the Loa remember who stole from them.
- **Break the Siege of Zul'Aman,** restore the Amani Empire and take your vengeance on Quel'Thalas.
- **New formable, the Amani Empire:** it reaches across Zul'Aman, the whole Ghostlands, Zul'Mashar, Northpass Tower and eastern Eversong. Hold 9 of its 13 states to proclaim it, and claim the rest.
- **Treasure hunters** keep trying their luck at the gates of Zul'Aman.

### Voices of Azeroth
- Every Quel'Thalas text has been reviewed and rewritten by hand: courtly High Elven prose for Silvermoon, broken and desperate whispers for the Wretched, and proper troll speech for the Amani, mon.
- New country names and adjectives for the realms of Quel'Thalas and the Ghostlands.
- New flags for Zul'Jarra and the Amani Empire.
- The first original event picture, a hand-drawn view of Eversong, kindly provided by a friend of the mod.

### Other changes
- Updated for patch 1.15.
- Horde and Alliance no longer fall apart at the start.
- Starting buildings, ports and trade centers for the Eastern Kingdoms, so the world market works again.
- Large error log cleanup and removal of outdated vanilla copies.

### Known limitations
- Many icons and event pictures are still vanilla placeholders.
- Costs, durations and Mana demand are a first balance pass. Feedback is very welcome.
- Not every branch has been played to the end, especially the late Wretched outcomes and the last Loa rituals. Please report anything that does not fire or looks wrong.
- The rest of the Eastern Kingdoms (Undercity, the Scarlet Crusade, Gilneas) comes next.
